## IPTV English channels: what should you verify before paying?

There is one part of IPTV shopping that should never be skipped: content rights.

IPTV itself is simply a technology for delivering television over the internet. The legal question depends on whether the provider has the appropriate rights to distribute the content. Ofcom explains the technology separately from the rules governing broadcast content, while UK government guidance warns that accessing subscription television, films or sports without the required permission or payment can constitute illicit streaming.

So, when evaluating any IPTV English channels service, check the provider's current terms and the rights situation in your country rather than assuming that a large channel list automatically means every listed feed is authorised.
